The Nandor Project
Project Overview
Complete renovation and smart/offgrid upgrade of a vintage motorhome.
A full ground-up rebuild of a 1984 Frontier Class C motorhome — stripping the interior back to shell and rebuilding it to a modern standard with quality materials, a clean earthy aesthetic, and every system purpose-designed for long-term off-grid living.
The goal is a self-sufficient mobile office and living space capable of operating entirely off-grid — solar-powered, digitally managed, and built to travel the BC coast south through to Mexico. The build covers a full interior redesign, mechanical upgrades throughout, and a custom smart home energy platform to monitor and manage everything on board.
